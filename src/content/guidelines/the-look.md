Every bambi plugin draws from one theme: light ground, black line work, structure by rules and alignment,
lowercase labels, tabular figures, no rounding, no shadows, no gradients.

## colour has meaning

On the controls, orange is the live value, and each plugin's own colour is what modulates, a source's signal
and a selection: the encoder's blue, echo's green, reverb's violet. In the scene, orange is what came in and
blue is what a plugin adds.

## one sign for chosen

Every tab and every choice draws the chosen option the same way: a black block with light text. The other
options are black text. Grey means only that something cannot be chosen.
